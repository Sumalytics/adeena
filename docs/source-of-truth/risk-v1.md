# RISK-v1

Status: implemented for the synthetic demo.

`publicationAllowed` is true only when rights are `cleared` and privacy is `no-identifiable-learner`. The demo always records rights as `unverified` and privacy as `no-identifiable-learner`, so publication stays disallowed. The schema rejects a record that disagrees with that rule.

No learner name, contact detail, or child account is collected.

Deferred proposal, not an approved product decision: a rights review that can record `cleared` or `blocked` from a verified source.
